Quick Installation Guide

Because Vibes is independently open source, here is how to install on mobile & desktop in 30 seconds.

Android & Windows

  1. Android: Download the .apk file. When prompted, tap "Settings" and toggle "Allow from this source" to complete the install.
  2. Windows: Download Vibes.exe. If Windows SmartScreen appears on first run, click "More info" then "Run anyway".

iPhone (iOS) & Mac

  1. iPhone: Download the .ipa and install it via Sideloadly (plug phone into PC/Mac, drag IPA into Sideloadly, click Start) or AltStore.
  2. macOS: Open Vibes.dmg and drag Vibes into Applications. If Gatekeeper blocks it, right-click the app and choose "Open".
