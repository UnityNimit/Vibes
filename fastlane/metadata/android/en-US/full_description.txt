**Vibes** is a lightweight, ad-free music player inspired by ViMusic, engineered with Rust (Tauri v2) and Svelte 5 for silky-smooth performance and minimal battery drain.

### 🌟 Key Features

- **Ad-Free Music Streaming**: Search and stream millions of tracks and albums directly from YouTube Music with zero commercial interruptions.
- **Playlist Import**: Import any YouTube or Spotify playlist with one click.
- **Automatic Offline Caching**: Streams audio while simultaneously caching chunks to local storage. Replaying your favorite songs requires 0 KB of mobile data and works fully offline.
- **Synchronized Karaoke Lyrics**: Real-time scrolling lyrics powered by LRCLIB. Tap any lyric line to jump directly to that exact part of the song.
- **Background Playback & Lockscreen Controls**: Native Android foreground media service with full lockscreen notifications and playback controls.
- **Ultra Lightweight & Battery Efficient**: Powered by native Rust background tasks and fine-grained reactive Svelte 5 runes. No heavy web view bloat.
- **100% Free, Open Source & Private**: No tracking, no ads, no telemetry, and zero proprietary Google Play Services dependencies. Works flawlessly on de-Googled Android devices (GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, LineageOS).
